# Product Price Calculator: Launch Narrative

## The Problem
For most independent makers, roasteries, DTC brands, and small manufacturers, pricing a new product is surprisingly stressful. Spreadsheets are clunky, hard to read on mobile, and prone to silent formula errors. A hidden percentage fee, an unaccounted 15% shrinkage during toll roasting, or packaging materials buried in the wrong row can instantly erase commercial margins. When customers ask for a quote, founders scramble through desktop files just to figure out their baseline cost.

## The Solution
Product Calculator is a modern, modular cost and margin engine designed with Apple-inspired craft. Instead of fighting nested spreadsheet cells, you build your price step by step using intuitive building blocks:
1. **Direct Fixed Costs**: Raw materials, processing fees, labor, or packaging.
2. **Dynamic Percentage Cascades**: Link fees to a cumulative subtotal (like import tariffs) or to a specific previous item (like 12% yield loss on green coffee).
3. **True Margin Solver**: Enter your desired gross profit margin (e.g. 25%), and the engine mathematically solves for the exact selling price needed to achieve that commercial return—not a naive markup mistake.
4. **Visual Waterfall Breakdown**: Every cost item is shown in a clean, proportional visual cascade, so you see exactly where every dollar or rupiah goes.

## Experience & Architecture
- **Distraction-Free Craft**: Built with Apple Design fluid physics, instant tactile feedback, and crystal-clear tabular figures that never cause layout shifts.
- **Global Currency Engine**: Seamlessly choose your base currency from USD ($), IDR (Rp), EUR (€), GBP (£), SGD (S$), JPY (¥), and more.
- **Dual-Storage Peace of Mind**: Works 100% offline via device storage, with optional Google OAuth and Cloudflare D1 database sync.
- **One-Tap Quotation Copy**: Copies a clean, professional price breakdown to your clipboard ready to send to clients or team members.
- **Live Production**: Deployed and instantly accessible on Vercel at `https://hitung-harga-product.vercel.app`.
